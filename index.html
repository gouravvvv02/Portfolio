Signal — Content Moderation Assistant

Live

An AI-powered content moderation tool that detects spam, phishing, and policy violations across 12 risk categories. Analyzes text instantly and assigns risk decisions (SPAM/SAFE/NEEDS REVIEW) with confidence scores and detailed reasoning.

Built from 1+ years of content moderation experience at TELUS International. Signal automates the exact spam detection patterns I apply daily—Telegram solicitation, IPTV piracy, gambling spam, crypto scams, phishing, and more.

Try Signal → Case Study ↓

How it works: Paste content → Signal analyzes against 200+ keywords across 12 categories → Returns decision badge, reasoning, detected flags, and confidence level. Works offline, no API keys needed.

Use cases: Reddit moderators, community managers, platform Trust & Safety teams, UGC platforms needing fast triage.

Signal Case Study

What: AI-powered content moderation tool that instantly detects spam, phishing, and policy violations across 12 risk categories.

Why: With 1+ years of experience in content moderation and Trust & Safety at TELUS International, I identified a gap: manual spam detection is repetitive and pattern-based. I built Signal to automate the exact judgment patterns I apply daily.

How: Uses a rule-based detection system with 200+ keywords organized into semantic categories (Telegram, IPTV, Betting, Crypto, Loans, Phishing, Adult Content, Work-from-Home scams, Counterfeits, and obfuscated text). Calculates risk scores and weights by severity.

Impact: Moderators can paste suspicious content and instantly get structured decisions. Perfect for any platform handling user-generated content.
